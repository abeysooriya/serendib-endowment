Annual reports

The numbers, once a year

Financial statements and activity reports, published after each annual general meeting, and the public registers where the entity can be verified.

The reporting cycle

Step one

Financial year ends

The financial year runs from 1 July to 30 June. Accounts are prepared after the year closes.

Step two

Annual general meeting

Held within five months of the end of the financial year. The directors present the financial statements and a report on the year's activities to members.

Step three

Published here

The report and statements are published on this page, and the chair's letter on the updates page.

Between reports

Contributors receive a personal update after each distribution to Sri Lanka: photographs from the ground, a note about the communities visited, and a record of what was given and to whom.

These updates are sent directly to contributors rather than published, and they are separate from the annual reporting above.

Updates and statements Governance

The Serendib Endowment acknowledges the Turrbal and Jagera peoples, the Traditional Owners of Meanjin (Brisbane), the land on which we work, and their custodianship of these lands and waters over tens of thousands of years. As an organisation dedicated to supporting and uplifting our community, we draw inspiration from this long tradition of mutual care and stewardship. We pay our deepest respects to Elders past, present and emerging, and extend that respect to all Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander peoples with us today.

The Serendib Endowment
