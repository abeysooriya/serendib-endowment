PUT THE PUBLISHED DOCUMENTS HERE
================================

The governance page links to two PDFs that must be placed in this folder, with
exactly these filenames:

  constitution.pdf
  investment-policy-statement.pdf

Until they are here, those links will 404. Either add the files or remove the
links from governance.html before publishing.

The other five policies are deliberately not published. The governance page
summarises what each covers; the documents themselves stay internal.

Annual reports also go here once they exist. See reports.html for the comment
marking where to add the link.
