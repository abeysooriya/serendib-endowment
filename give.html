A gift that does not get spent

What happens after you give

01
Your gift joins the corpus

It is recorded, added to the permanent capital of the fund, and invested under the Investment Policy Statement. It is not spent.

02
You are listed, if you want to be

Contributors are named below with permission and amounts are kept private. Let us know if you would prefer to remain anonymous.

03
You hear what the returns did

A personal update after each distribution to Sri Lanka, and once a year the chair's letter and the annual report covering returns, distributions and the corpus.
